Dario Esposito

PhD Candidate in Economics, Bocconi University

I am a PhD candidate in Economics at Bocconi University, where I also obtained my MSc degree. My research interests are primarily in macroeconomics, business cycle analysis, fiscal and monetary policy, and time series econometrics. Moreover, I am interested in exploring machine learning applications in forecasting and structural analysis.

Before starting my PhD, I have worked for over two years in DG-Economics at the European Central Bank and the Bank of Italy, where I collaborated on research projects in business cycle analysis, labour and international macroeconomics, and forecasting.

You can reach me at dario.esposito@phd.unibocconi.it.
